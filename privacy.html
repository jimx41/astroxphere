Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Astrozphere has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of ours that store information about you. Most of what the app remembers stays on your phone. This page explains the few places your data goes and why.

Delete your data (Astrozphere by Z Sphere)

How to request deletion: email itsjimlive@gmail.com with the subject "Astrozphere data deletion" and we will reply with what we can do and, where another service holds the data, who to contact.

Data stored on your phone (saved places, game scores, settings, your Premium status, cached news and forecasts): you can delete all of it yourself at any time. Open Android Settings, then Apps, then Astrozphere, then Storage, then "Clear storage", or uninstall the app. We do not have a copy of it.

Data we keep: we keep no account and no personal data about you on our own servers. The only thing we may hold is an email you send us, which we keep only as long as needed to reply, and delete on request.

Data held by other services (Google AdMob for ads, RevenueCat for purchases, and the weather and launch data sources) follows their own retention rules, described in section 4. You can reset your advertising ID in Android settings, and you can ask us to point you to the right service.

1. Who we are

Astrozphere (package com.zsphere.astroxphere) is an Android app made by Z Sphere, an independent developer based in Bangladesh. In this policy, "we", "us" and "the app" mean Z Sphere and Astrozphere. You can reach us at itsjimlive@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

The app saves these things only on your phone, using the app's local storage. We cannot see them:

  • Places you save for Sky Weather and the ISS Tracker (a name and coordinates), and which one is selected
  • Your best Space Shooter score, chosen ship, and sound setting
  • Whether Premium is unlocked, and whether you have seen the welcome screens
  • Saved copies of recent news, launch lists, forecasts, the Astronomy Picture of the Day, and ISS orbit data, so the app works faster and offline

Uninstalling the app, or choosing "Clear storage" for it in Android settings, deletes all of this.

3. Location

The app never asks for your location when it opens. It asks only when you tap Use my location in Sky Weather or the ISS Tracker. You can instead type a place or coordinates, or pick a famous observatory, and never grant any permission.

If you do share it, your coordinates are used to work out tonight's sky, the Moon and Sun positions, and ISS passes. Those calculations run on your phone. To get the weather forecast, the app sends the coordinates (rounded to 3 decimal places, plus altitude) to MET Norway, described below. We do not receive or store your location, and the app does not track your location in the background.

5. Ads

The free version of Astrozphere shows ads provided by Google AdMob (full-screen ads between Space Shooter games and a small banner on the home screen). AdMob may collect and use device identifiers such as your Android advertising ID, your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads, to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. This may include personalized ads, depending on your Google settings and where you live. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads. Learn more at how Google uses data in ads. People who unlock Premium do not see ads.

6. Purchases

Premium is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether a purchase is active and to restore it on a new device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase information such as which product was bought and when; see the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. Google handles your payment details. We never see or store your card or payment information.

7. Feedback you send us

The "Help shape Astrozphere" screen lets you suggest a feature, request a story, or report a problem. When you press Send, the app opens your own email app with your message ready to go, addressed to itsjimlive@gmail.com. Nothing is sent until you send the email yourself. Your message arrives with your email address and anything you wrote, plus the app version and the word "android". We use it only to read your feedback and reply, and we do not share it. Please do not put passwords or private information in a message. You can ask us to delete an email by writing to us.

8. What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account or give your name, email or phone number.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not track your location in the background.
  • We do not run our own servers that collect data about you.
  • We do not use your data to build a profile of you.

9. Children

Astrozphere is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will help.

10. Your choices and rights

Because the app keeps your data on your own phone, you control it: delete a saved place inside the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall it. You can turn location permission on or off in Android settings at any time, and you can reset or limit your advertising ID as described above. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held by the services in section 4; contact those services directly, or email us and we will point you the right way.

11. Security

The app connects to services over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we keep the amount of data the app handles to a minimum.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its "Last updated" date. Please check back from time to time.
